# FSI 评分系统更新汇报 / FSI Scoring System Update Report

---

## 开场 / Opening

中文：本周我们对 FSI 评分系统做了一轮比较彻底的更新，目标是让分数更合理、让地图颜色真实反映评分、让整个系统更稳定也更流畅。

EN: This week we made a thorough update to the FSI scoring system. Our goals were to make the scores more reasonable, to make the map colors truly reflect those scores, and to make the whole system more stable and responsive.

---

## 一、评分维度精简 / Dimension Simplification

中文：我们原本有三个评分维度，分别是商业、社交、便利设施。

EN: We originally had three scoring dimensions: Commercial, Social, and Amenity.

中文：但是在实际数据中我们发现，便利设施和商业的 POI 高度重叠，分成两个维度反而让含义变得模糊。

EN: However, in the real data we found that Amenity and Commercial POIs overlapped heavily, and splitting them actually made the meaning less clear.

中文：所以我们把便利设施合并进了商业维度，现在只保留两个维度：商业和社交。

EN: So we merged Amenity into Commercial. Now we keep only two dimensions: Commercial and Social.

中文：一条街的总分，等于这两个维度中较高的那一个。

EN: A street's total score equals whichever of the two dimensions is higher.

中文：这样处理是因为我们认为，一条街只要有一个明确的主要用途足够强，它就有被激活的价值，不应该因为另一个维度弱就被拉低。

EN: We do it this way because we believe a street with one clear strong purpose deserves to be activated, and should not be dragged down just because the other dimension is weak.

---

## 二、评分能真正拉开差距 / Scores Now Spread Properly

中文：之前的问题是，地图上的街道要么全部红色，要么全部绿色，中间的差距完全看不出来。

EN: The earlier problem was that streets on the map were either all red or all green, and the middle ground was invisible.

中文：我们重新调整了 POI 密度曲线，让费城真实的分布可以自然铺开成一个从红到橙、到黄、再到绿的完整梯度。

EN: We retuned the POI density curve so that Philadelphia's real distribution naturally spreads into a full gradient from red, to orange, to yellow, to green.

中文：现在一条街要拿到 90 分以上，需要在五分钟步行范围内有大约二十个相关的 POI。

EN: Now for a street to score 90 or higher, it needs roughly twenty relevant POIs within a five-minute walk.

---

## 三、交通规则重新设计 / Traffic Logic Rewritten

中文：在旧的交通规则下，大多数街道在数学上就注定永远到不了 90 分，天花板定得太低了。

EN: Under the old traffic rules, most streets were mathematically destined never to reach 90. The ceiling was simply set too low.

中文：我们重写了规则，让只要 POI 足够密集的好街道，能够真正突破 90 分的门槛。

EN: We rewrote the rules so that streets with dense POIs can genuinely break through the 90 threshold.

中文：同时，高速公路和由州政府管辖的道路仍然会被严格惩罚,它们本来就不适合关闭。

EN: At the same time, highways and state-managed roads are still heavily penalised, because they genuinely are not suitable for closure.

中文：这样的设计既符合常识直觉，也能真正把有潜力的优质街道筛选出来。

EN: This design matches common-sense intuition, and also genuinely surfaces the streets with real potential.

---

## 四、加入了真实时间因素 / Real-Time Factors Added

中文：评分现在会根据当下的真实情况动态调整，我们加入了四个外部因素。

EN: The score now dynamically adjusts based on current real-world conditions. We added four external factors.

中文：第一个是时间段。一天被分为早、午、晚、夜四个时段，不同时段的交通惩罚不一样。

EN: The first is time-of-day. We divide the day into morning, afternoon, evening, and night, and the traffic penalty differs for each.

中文：第二个是天气。我们接入了 Open-Meteo 这个免费天气服务，拉取费城的实时天气。晴天没有惩罚，雨天打六折，雷暴打三折，下雪打两折。

EN: The second is weather. We integrated Open-Meteo, a free weather service, to pull live Philadelphia weather. Clear weather gets no penalty, rain gets a 40 percent reduction, thunderstorms get a 70 percent reduction, and snow gets an 80 percent reduction.

中文：第三个是大型活动。我们接入了 Ticketmaster,获取费城十英里范围内未来七天的演出和比赛,离场馆越近的街道获得的加成越高。

EN: The third is large events. We integrated Ticketmaster to pull concerts and games within ten miles of Philadelphia over the next seven days. Streets closer to venues receive bigger boosts.

中文：第四个是节假日。我们内置了费城的十个重要节日，包括独立日、感恩节游行、费城马拉松、圣诞市集等等,这些日子当天全城所有街道都会获得加成。

EN: The fourth is holidays. We built in ten important Philadelphia holidays, including Independence Day, the Thanksgiving Day Parade, the Philadelphia Marathon, and Christmas Village Season. On those days, every street in the city receives a boost.

中文：这四个因素共同决定一条街在"此时此刻"到底值不值得关闭。

EN: These four factors together determine whether a street is worth closing right now, at this very moment.

---

## 五、彻底去除了随机成分 / All Randomness Removed

中文：之前系统里有一个隐藏的问题：地图上的颜色其实是用街道 ID 做哈希算出来的假分数，根本和真实的 FSI 没关系。

EN: There was a hidden problem in the old system: the colors on the map were actually pseudo-scores generated by hashing the street ID, and had no relationship to the real FSI at all.

中文：我们把所有这类伪随机的代码全部删除,现在每一个颜色都来自真实的评分计算。

EN: We removed all such pseudo-random code. Now every color comes from a real scoring computation.

中文：如果一条街还没有数据，它就显示灰色，而不是瞎猜一个颜色。

EN: If a street has no data yet, it is shown in gray, rather than being assigned a guessed color.

中文：面板上显示的分数和地图上的颜色现在完全一致，不会再出现两边对不上的情况。

EN: The score shown in the panel and the color shown on the map now match exactly, and the two can no longer drift apart.

---

## 六、修复了几个让人困惑的问题 / Confusing Bugs Fixed

中文：第一个问题是，有一条 81 分的街道在地图上显示为绿色，看起来应该是 90 分以上。

EN: The first issue was that a street scoring 81 was showing up green on the map, as if it were above 90.

中文：原因是地图上色的时候忽略了 AI 分数这一项，我们已经把它补回了计算链路。

EN: The cause was that the map color computation had skipped the AI score. We have now added it back into the pipeline.

中文：第二个问题是,同一条街放大和缩小会显示不同的分数。

EN: The second issue was that the same street could show different scores at different zoom levels.

中文：原因是我们之前用的是线段中点作为采样点,放大缩小时会取到不同的点。我们现在改用整条街的几何中心，任何缩放级别下都是同一个坐标。

EN: The cause was that we were using the midpoint of the line segment as the sampling point, which shifted when zooming. We now use the true centroid of the entire street, which is the same coordinate at any zoom level.

中文：第三个问题是,多段式的长街道被代码直接跳过了，完全没有评分。

EN: The third issue was that multi-segment long streets were being skipped entirely by the code, and received no score at all.

中文：我们补全了对这种几何类型的处理。

EN: We have now added proper handling for this geometry type.

---

## 七、性能优化 / Performance Optimisation

中文：做完前面这些改动之后，地图变得很卡。

EN: After all the above changes were made, the map became severely laggy.

中文：原因是每次缩放或者平移停下之后,程序都会一次性同步计算几百条街道,阻塞了主线程。

EN: The cause was that every time panning or zooming stopped, the program was synchronously computing hundreds of streets at once, blocking the main thread.

中文：我们的解决办法是分帧渲染加上版本号缓存：已经算过的街道不会重复计算;新的一批计算如果中途用户又缩放了,会立刻中断换新的一批。

EN: Our solution is frame-by-frame rendering plus versioned caching. Already-computed streets are not recomputed, and if the user zooms again mid-batch, the current batch aborts immediately and a new one takes over.

中文：现在平移和缩放都非常流畅，没有卡顿。

EN: Panning and zooming are now very smooth, with no lag.

---

## 八、验证准确率 / Validation Accuracy

中文：为了衡量模型好不好，我们把模型打出 90 分以上的街道，和费城官方的 Play Streets 和 Open Streets 实际项目做了对比。

EN: To measure how well the model works, we compared the streets our model scored 90 or higher against Philadelphia's official Play Streets and Open Streets programs.

中文：整个校准过程经历了几轮调整，从一开始的 1% 吻合度,到过度调整后的 99%,最终稳定在大约 85%。

EN: The calibration went through several rounds, starting at 1 percent agreement, overshooting to 99 percent, and finally stabilising at around 85 percent.

中文：85% 这个数字是有意义的,既不是靠把阈值调松凑出来的虚假重合，也不是过于严格到漏掉官方街道。

EN: This 85 percent figure is meaningful. It is not a fake overlap achieved by loosening the threshold, nor is it so strict that it misses the city's own picks.

---

## 九、整体流程总结 / End-to-End Pipeline

中文：一条街的最终得分,等于 POI 密度乘以 AI 加权，再乘以审批惩罚，再依次乘上时间、天气、活动、节假日这几个实时因素。

EN: A street's final score equals its POI density, weighted with the AI score, multiplied by the permit-approval penalty, then multiplied in turn by time-of-day, weather, events, and holidays.

中文：任何一项因素的变化，比如用户切换了时段、天气更新了、新的活动被拉到，都会立刻触发地图自动重新计算。

EN: Any change to any factor — the user switching time bins, a weather refresh, a new batch of events — immediately triggers an automatic recomputation across the map.

---

## 十、可演示的改进点 / What to Demo

中文：我来演示五个直观的改进点。

EN: I will demonstrate five intuitive improvements.

中文：第一，切换早、中、晚、夜四个时段，地图颜色会随之变化。

EN: First, switching between morning, afternoon, evening, and night, the map colors change accordingly.

中文：第二，选择一个节假日,和选择一个普通工作日做对比，节假日整个城市的分数会整体抬高。

EN: Second, picking a holiday versus a regular weekday, the entire city's scores rise on the holiday.

中文：第三，点击任意一条街，面板显示的分数会和地图上的颜色区间完全对应。

EN: Third, clicking on any street, the score shown in the panel corresponds exactly to the color band on the map.

中文：第四，任意放大、缩小、拖动地图，整个过程非常流畅，没有卡顿。

EN: Fourth, zooming, panning, and dragging the map is completely smooth, with no lag.

中文：第五，屏幕右上角的验证徽章,实时显示我们的模型和费城官方项目之间的吻合率。

EN: Fifth, the validation badge in the top-right corner shows in real time the agreement rate between our model and Philadelphia's official programs.

EN: That concludes this week's FSI system updates. Thank you.
